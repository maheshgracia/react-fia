<p dir="ltr" style="color: rgb(0,0,0); line-height: 1.38; margin-top: 0pt; margin-bottom: 0pt;"><span style="font-size: 11pt; font-family: Arial,sans-serif; color: rgb(31,31,31); font-variant-ligatures: normal; font-variant-alternates: normal; font-variant-numeric: normal; font-variant-east-asian: normal; vertical-align: baseline; white-space: pre-wrap;">The record-breaking display featured 1,383 U.S. flags arranged on flagpoles to form a massive number "250," encircled by Indian tricolors. The arrangement commemorated a unique dual anniversary: India&rsquo;s 80th Independence Day (India@80) and the United States' upcoming Semiquincentennial (America@250). Following meticulous verification on-site, the official Guinness World Records Adjudicator presented the framed certificate to organization leaders and dignitaries.</span></p>
